FULL DESCRIPTION (4000 chars max — paste as-is)
-------------------------------------------------
GuidePaw Companion is the official mobile app for GuidePaw — the all-in-one platform built for service dog handlers, trainers, and visually impaired individuals who rely on their dogs every day.

Whether you're logging training sessions, managing vet appointments, or preparing for travel, GuidePaw Companion puts everything in one place — designed to be simple, accessible, and always with you.

─────────────────────────────────────
WHAT'S INCLUDED
─────────────────────────────────────

🐾 Training Log
Track every session with notes, duration, exercises, and media attachments. Build a complete history you can share with your trainer or certifying organization.

📋 Health & Vet Records
Log medications, appointments, and health documents. Never lose track of vaccines, check-ups, or prescriptions.

🆔 ADA Access Card
Generate a digital ADA information card for your service dog — ready to show at restaurants, hotels, and businesses.

✈️ Air Travel Prep
Step-by-step airline documentation checklists built for DOT compliance.

🏠 Housing Rights FAQ
Know your rights under the Fair Housing Act. Built-in guidance for service dog handlers navigating housing situations.

📍 QR Dog Tag Tracking
See when and where your dog's QR tag is scanned. If someone finds your dog, they can submit a location report directly through the public QR profile.

🔔 Smart Alerts
Get notified about important events — vaccination due dates, training milestones, and more.

🤝 Trainer Marketplace
Connect with certified service dog trainers linked to your dog's profile.

🎯 Goal Builder & Habit Repair
Set training goals and track habit correction progress week by week.
