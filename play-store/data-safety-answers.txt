===============================================================
GUIDEPAW COMPANION — DATA SAFETY FORM ANSWERS
Play Console → App content → Data safety
Answer each question as shown below.
===============================================================

SECTION 1: Data collection and security
----------------------------------------
Q: Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types?
A: YES

Q: Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit?
A: YES

Q: Do you provide a way for users to request that their data is deleted?
A: YES  (via email to admin@guidepaw.app, stated in privacy policy)


SECTION 2: Data types collected
---------------------------------

── LOCATION ──
Approximate location:  YES — collected, not shared
Precise location:      YES — collected, not shared
  Purpose: App functionality (navigation features, found-dog location reports)
  Required or optional: Optional (user grants permission)
  Collected in background: NO

── PERSONAL INFO ──
Name:           YES — collected, not shared
Email address:  YES — collected, not shared
User IDs:       YES — collected, not shared
  Purpose: Account management, app functionality
  Required: YES

── HEALTH AND FITNESS ──
Health info:    YES — collected, not shared
  (vet records, medications, health documents for the service dog)
  Purpose: App functionality
  Required: Optional

── PHOTOS AND VIDEOS ──
Photos:         YES — collected, not shared
  (dog profile photos, training media attachments)
  Purpose: App functionality
  Required: Optional

── AUDIO ──
Audio files:    YES — collected, not shared
  (training session audio attachments)
  Purpose: App functionality
  Required: Optional

── FILES AND DOCS ──
Files and docs: YES — collected, not shared
  (certification documents, vet records PDFs)
  Purpose: App functionality
  Required: Optional

── APP ACTIVITY ──
App interactions: YES — collected, not shared
  Purpose: Analytics (aggregate, anonymized via Google Analytics 4)
  Required: YES

── DEVICE OR OTHER IDs ──
Device or other IDs: NO


SECTION 3: Data sharing
------------------------
Q: Does your app share any of the collected data with third parties?
A: NO  (payment processing via Stripe is initiated outside the app in the system browser — no payment data passes through the app)


SECTION 4: Data handling practices
------------------------------------
Q: Is the data collected required for your app, or can users choose whether it's collected?
A: Location, photos, audio, health info — OPTIONAL
   Name, email, user IDs — REQUIRED for account


NOTES FOR REVIEWER
-------------------
- Camera is used to take dog photos and scan QR codes. No camera data is stored or transmitted without explicit user action.
- Location is never collected in the background.
- No payment card data ever passes through the app. All purchases open guidepaw.app in the system browser.
